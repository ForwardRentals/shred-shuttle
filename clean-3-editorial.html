Good to know

Frequently asked

How do I book multiple laps?
Pick your first time slot, continue to the personal-info page, then choose "add to booking" to stack more laps. For beginners we recommend every other hour unless you know the trails — hourly only works if you can finish the descent in 30–35 min.
Can I bring an e-bike?
Yes — but the rider loads and secures their own bike and must be satisfied before we roll. Our racks are built for regular bikes, so damage from the extra weight isn't the responsibility of the Shred Shuttle or its staff.
Do I need a reservation?
For ski & snowboard shuttles, yes — we don't stop where no reservation is made, and booking is online only. For MTB laps we strongly recommend booking ahead so your seat and your bike's spot are locked in.
What's the cancellation policy?
MTB & ski: cancel 24 hrs before departure for 75% back, or 12 hrs before for 50% — later cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable. Multi-ride passes are non-refundable but transferable. Private & wedding bookings take a 50% non-refundable deposit; multi-day trips 30%. All changes need an email with booking ID, name, date and time.
Where do the MTB shuttles run?
Diamond Head, above Quest University. Upper: Meadow of the Grizzly & 19th Hole. Middle: Half Nelson, Angry Midget, Ditch Pig. Lower: Pseudo Tsuga & Lowdown. Main pickup is the Pseudo Tsuga lot — first parking area on Mamquam Rd above Quest.
How long is the shuttle up?
About 20–25 minutes up roughly 900 m. A full MOG → Half Nelson → Pseudo Tsuga descent takes around 1 hr 15 min.
